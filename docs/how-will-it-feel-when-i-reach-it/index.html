How Will It Feel When I Reach It?

October 17, 2026 · 2 min read

I started this EECS program not too long ago. But I’ve been thinking about the day I get a SWE job or finish the program. How will that feel?

The past few years, it’s felt like I’ve been coasting through life. I always felt a draw to software programming, but I could never follow through. Then I realized I hadn’t completed or accomplished anything. I’ve never finished anything I’ve started, and that eats at me. Will that feeling get better as I move through the course? But I want to focus on this: how will it feel when I reach it?

First, I think it will only increase my sense of purpose. Right now it feels like I have gas in the tank I’m not using. But it turns out I am using it. I’m using that fuel to haunt my own thoughts. If I don’t use that fuel, my mind will: by beating myself up, comparing myself to peers, or just bingeing the fuel away. What happens if I use all my fuel? Will I be happy with myself? Will those bad habits still have fuel to run on? If my fuel goes to my purpose, then my purpose will refill my fuel.

Ultimately, I started this because I felt “dumb.” I didn’t have great hearing growing up, so I was in speech therapy until 8th grade. I could barely talk, because I couldn’t hear until I was 6. I had a super hard time sounding out words, because I couldn’t even make the same sounds as everyone else. So, honestly, I never really learned how to spell. And because I couldn’t spell, I drifted away from English classes, which pushed me even further behind. But I believe that once I get these skills, I’ll only get better. I’ll be more confident in my English and writing, which will make me want to write more, and that’s the goal.

Lastly, from all that, you can probably guess that I feel useless right now. I’ve been hard-stuck in fast food for 3 years, in my late 20s, and it sucks. I’m in my “prime.” And since it’s 2026 and AI is advancing like crazy, feeling useless == feeling replaceable by AI. Nobody wants to feel like they can’t contribute to society.

Looking at how I’ve felt in the past, I think, shows me how I’ll feel after completing something like this. I really feel the purpose part. I want to drive toward something bigger. I really believe any human has the capacity to do anything in life. Then I’ll never have to feel dumb or useless again. I’ll know what I studied and how hard I worked to reach this goal. Shoot, I already feel proud of the little progress I’ve made so far. I’m doing it. I’m going to reach it. I’m finally going to complete something!
