What I'm doing now, and why✳

This is my /now page: what I'm focused on at this point in my life. Last updated 2026-10-18.

NOW.TXT2026-10-18

Right now

  • Working as a Produce Clerk at Kroger.
  • Studying English and Math: the foundations everything else is built on.
  • Writing every day. 3 pieces published so far.
GOALS.TXT★

What I'm working toward

  • A software engineering job: the first real milestone, and the one I'm working toward now.
  • A full EECS education: the equivalent of MIT's 6-3 degree plus a master's: 54 courses and roughly 6,600 hours, from arithmetic and grammar up through systems, hardware and a capstone.
  • The habit of finishing: one lesson, one page and one project at a time.
WHY.TXT♥

Why I'm doing this

  1. I want to finish something. I've always been drawn to programming, but I never followed through. I've never finished anything I started, and that eats at me. This is the one I finish.
  2. I want to stop feeling like the "dumb" one. I couldn't hear until I was six and was in speech therapy until 8th grade. I never really learned to spell, so I stayed away from writing. Now I study the basics again and write every day, in public.
  3. I have more to give. I spent years in fast food and package handling. I'm good at running a shift, but I've had gas in the tank I wasn't using. With AI moving this fast, I want to be someone who builds the future, not someone it replaces.
  4. Purpose fuels me. When my energy has nowhere to go, it turns into beating myself up. When it goes into something bigger, it comes back.

“If my fuel goes to my purpose, then my purpose is going to fill my fuel.”

me, in How Will It Feel When I Reach It?
