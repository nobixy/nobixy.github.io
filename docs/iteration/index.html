A NOTE FROM THE WORKBENCH ✳

Iteration

October 12, 2026✳5 min read

There I was. It was a hot summer going into a breathtaking fall. I wanted to go grab a pumpkin from the grocery store and decorate it. So I put on my shoes, walked out the door, and made sure I had my phone and necklace. I never leave without them anymore. You may ask why? Am I that popular?

The truth is, I was doing some homebrewing at my house. It’s 2026, so of course I have AI doing all the heavy lifting, and I’m just here to verify the overall structure. But I was developing this AI necklace that’s like an AI companion. But this is where stuff gets weird. The AI put something on my phone and the necklace. I tried to review the code, but I can’t even read it. It looks like it’s some sorta app, called “Hocus Pocus”. I opened it. It already had a dashboard open. I think, “But this is my first time opening it.” I let that thought drift as I was more intrigued, more interested in exploring what this is. It looks like it’s an MMORPG but for real life. Those stats are for anything you can think of. Then I see a stat maxed out at “100/100 imagination.” I thought that was weird, as I didn’t think I was any more or less imaginative than any other person.

I closed the app as I was walking down the steps to leave. I live on “Main Street”. So I’m really close to everything, including the grocery store. As I’m making my way down the street. I see some of my favorite people. Itdo the shopkeeper that could make you laugh on your worst days. Then Mrs. Johnson in the shop right next to his. She owned the local gaming cafe where I ended up every day after school. I pretty much grew up there. I know, without her, I wouldn’t be as into homebrewing as I am today.

But right as I’m waving to her, I see my buddy Jack running for me. Then, when he gets close, he yells into his phone, “water blast,” and out of thin air, an impossible blast of water shoots out of his phone. I’m instantly shocked and dripping wet. But he drops his phone, and the water stops. I ask him what the hell that was for. He is just laughing, rolling on the ground. As he does, I try to understand what just happened. I say out loud, “HOW DID YOU DO THAT? THAT’S AN IMPOSSIBLE AMOUNT OF WATER!” Jack is still trying to catch your breath from laughing. He finally gets up. “You’re on the app, right?” I ask, “What the hell are you talking about?” He looks down and says, “Oh, I thought I saw you on an app I have, but I’m not allowed to talk about it, unless you’re also on the app.” I ask, “What’s it called?” he replies, “I can’t say; it just automatically downloads to random phones.

No one knows how many people are on it. But estimates are around 3000 people. He pulls out his phone. Then shows me on a weird 3D-ish map. That’s how he knew I downloaded it. I pull out my phone to see, and he’s on my map too. I zoom out of the map. Then I ask, “Why can’t I see everyone?” He replies, “It’s a distance parameter. You have to be ‘x’ feet away before people show up.” I’ve only ever seen 3 others, not including you. But I was scared, so I ran as far as I could. But today I saw the map populate, then I saw you, so I took my shot ,hoping it was you.

I began clicking through all the options. Then, when I saw Duel, I clicked on Jack on the map. I asked if he’d ever done it. He said, “No, want to?” I replied, “Of course”. I clicked Duel, and it put us in a match. Everything around us changes to this chromatic representation of the real world. Jack and I were pushed 50 feet apart. I yelled, “What’s going on?” He replied, “I know just as much as you now.”

An echoing voice bounced off the wall, saying, “You may use any magic in this match, and to BEGIN!” Jack didn’t waste any time; he hit me with another water blast. This time it felt stronger. It was hard to keep my feet planted. While I was holding my breath. As the water stopped dripping off me, I yelled, “How do I know what magic I have?” Jack replied, “It’s the bookshelf tab, then spells.” Of course, I go straight for the table of contents, looking for a crazy spell. Found it, I said. I begin to yell, “Fearsome, - “, Jack cuts me off. “HAHAHA, you can’t cast that. Look, it says right here this is a legendary spell. You would need to be at least max level, and even then I think it’s still a myth.” I say fuck it, “FEARSOME, THE ANCIENT DRAGON” Out of nowhere, a highly polite 19th-century British man shows up in the middle of us. Jack just starts laughing because he’s literally in back and white. He slowly opens his briefcase, and the red-yellowish glow is emerging and engulfing the chromium environment. As it gets brighter, it gets so blinding I close my eyes. Even with my eyes closed, I see red like a picture in my head. Then the jets of wind are swirling all the air around, causing so much turbulence that I can barely stay on my feet. I feel a hand grab me to keep me from falling. I think, “That must be Jack.” Then, just as fast as it happened, it stopped, and I opened my eyes, and it was black. I couldn’t see anything. All I could see was that it wasn’t Jack holding me up; it was the man.
