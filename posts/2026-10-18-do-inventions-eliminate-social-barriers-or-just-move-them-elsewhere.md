Living in 2026, you couldn't imagine finding the top floors of building undesirable. But in early 19th century that was the case. For example, the top level in a lot of building used to be "the help": cooks, and maids. While the wealthy residents lived on the bottom floors. You might find this odd, but what invention changed this dynamic. The  elevator, it made it easier to move between floor.

Before the elevator, the wealthy didn't want to clime a fleet of stairs to get to their home. So this made residents prefer the lower floors (commonly on the second floor) for ease of access. while the poorer residents got the higher floors with the views. The elevator made it much easier to transport people vertically in buildings. They also come with the a smaller footprint. which means the more floor space. This made the rich change their view on what luxury was. It was no longer the ease of access. It was the privacy and views you can get while also being convenient to get to.

Another invention that had a similar effect was the automobile. A lot of wealthy people in the USA lived in cities before the first car was developed. Mostly for the same reasons as the elevator; ease of access. Anything they need is just a few minutes away. While the elevator made it easier to move vertically. The first cars made it easier to move horizontally. People could now live farther. While still having the same convenient stores only a few minutes by car. This sparked what is now known as suburban living. 

The possibilities created by these inventions was immerse. The car made it possible to keep your high-paying city job, while living in a lower cost part of town. This also made it possible to get away from the city-life and noise. But made it easy to go back to at any point. This made you home life more peaceful. The commute to and from work was faster. The elevator made it where it didn't have to feel like you ran a marathon to get home. Then don't even talk to me if you have to bring groceries home too. But on the other hand, the consequences are felt in poor people kept out of their homes. Because, it became more valuable to rent to someone else. Rural communities that were demolished to make way for suburban development. While these inventions made us much more productive that also caused hurt as well. 

I was watching a video someone looking at the smallest houses/apartments in Paris. I realized they are mostly on the top floor. I found it odd, as usually the top floors are for penthouses. For the, view and privacy that come with that. But then, I realized most of these building didn't have elevators. Also, that penthouse were a product of the elevator even existing. This is all the say in a round about way that. Making something physically possible is not the same as making it socially or economically accessible.
