Rules of the House
  1. Climb, don't leap. Prompt, freewrite, one-sentence point, plan, draft. New shows the steps.
  2. Drafting and revising are different jobs. Draft mode hides every check so you can get it down.
  3. Short, regular sessions beat binges. A 15-30 min sprint, most days.
  4. Freewrite when stuck. Turn on "no backspace" and keep the pen moving.
  5. Revise big before small. Check the Outline first, then the sentences.
  6. Revisit old prompts. Same question, months apart. The Journal shows how your thinking moved.
  7. Read it out loud. Your ear catches what your eye skips.
  8. Orwell: short words, cut what you can, active voice, no worn-out phrases. Break any rule before writing something ugly.
